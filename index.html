1. AI-Based System for Residential Energy Efficiency Optimization

Project overview: Developed a generative AI system that analyzes household energy conditions and produces practical energy-saving recommendations. The work used the Appliances Energy Prediction dataset and transformed energy, temperature, humidity, and weather measurements into structured prompt-response examples.

Tools and technologies: Python, Pandas, GPT-2, Hugging Face Transformers, LoRA/PEFT, PyTorch, and Streamlit.

  • Generated household-specific energy-efficiency suggestions.
  • Evaluated response format, relevance, and hallucination risk.
  • Designed the solution for an interactive Streamlit interface.

2. Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

Project overview: Built and compared machine-learning models using a telecom dataset containing 7,043 customer records and 21 original columns. Data preparation included missing-value handling, categorical encoding, scaling, feature engineering, stratified splitting, and SMOTE on training data only.

Tools and technologies: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, imbalanced-learn, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

  • Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost.
  • Logistic Regression achieved ROC-AUC of approximately 0.837 and recall of approximately 0.738.
  • Random Forest achieved the highest accuracy at approximately 0.766.

5. U.S. Retail Sales Forecasting Using SARIMA

Project overview: Developed a SARIMA time-series model to forecast monthly U.S. retail sales. Historical data from January 1992 through June 2020 was used for training, while July 2020 through June 2021 was reserved for testing.

Tools and technologies: Python, Pandas, Statsmodels, Matplotlib, and time-series analysis.

  • Analyzed trend and seasonal behavior.
  • Produced forecasts with confidence intervals.
  • Achieved a test RMSE of approximately 59,819.
